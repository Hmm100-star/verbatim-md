# Negative blocks
## Framework responses
### Against agency alone
:::analytic
```text
Ask why the proposed criterion should outweigh fairness. Distinguish a person's
ability to choose from the distribution of meaningful opportunities to choose.
These are analytical prompts, not source quotations.
```
:::
#### Opportunity affects the practical meaning of choice
